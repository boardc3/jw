## 1. Snapshot

Julia Warter is a Costa Mesa, CA wellness creator who turned an eight-year health crisis (IBS, gallbladder sludge, a 19 cm enlarged liver, and a "massive pre-cancerous" colon polyp found at 29) into a root-cause content brand built on lymphatic drainage, fascia release, and gut health. She is not a clinician — she has a CSULB business-marketing degree and previously founded Wander Hat Company — and her authority is lived experience plus "$20,000 out of pocket on specialists," which is the emotional core of everything she publishes.

## 2. Voice & tone

Warm, direct, self-deprecating friend who "fearlessly" covers "uncomfortable health topics" (ilovecreatives). Short one-line paragraphs, bold key claims, occasional ALL CAPS ("GLOWED"), exclamation points.

- **Journey hook:** "If you've been following my journey for a while, you know my gut health has been a long, delicate process."
- **Reader-question frame:** "One question I get all the time is, 'What protein powder do you use?'" / "What probiotic do you actually take?"
- **Visceral stakes:** "When I ate garlic and onion I looked nine months pregnant." / "there were days I looked several months pregnant by the afternoon." / "Some days, my symptoms were so severe that I couldn't even leave my home."
- **"Let's be real" + brutal-honesty framing:** "Here is my brutally honest breakdown of the experience."
- **Root-cause mantra:** "instead of simply masking them," "looking at the body as a whole," "true vitality isn't about extreme detoxes or overnight miracles," "I'm never looking for a quick fix."
- **Sensory symptom words:** "puffy, heavy, and stuck," "fluid just sits there," "sluggish," "stagnant," "cellular flush." The LRM pitch line: "It could be a movement problem."
- **Fragment cadence:** "Slow. Patient. Consistent." / "Chicken. Fish. Eggs." / "Healed my liver." / "Reduced stress."
- **Self-deprecating asides:** "(by most, I mean me)," "I am embarrassed to say," "still pinching myself."
- **Humility guardrails:** "I am not a doctor or a nutritionist," "no question is too embarrassing," "it did not cure my IBS," "highly individual."
- **Sign-offs:** "Xo, Julia" (standard); "Your gut (and your skin!) will thank you"; "As always, listen to your body, prioritize real whole foods…"; "I can't wait to hear your story."
