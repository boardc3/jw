## Why I'm So Careful About Protein Powders

When you have IBS, your digestive system doesn't always appreciate heavily processed ingredients, artificial sweeteners, gums, fillers, or long ingredient lists.

Over the years, I've learned that the simpler I keep my nutrition, the better my gut feels.

Many protein powders contain ingredients that leave me bloated or uncomfortable. Even when they taste great, I often end up paying for it later.

Organised Protein is different.

It's made with real, whole-food ingredients that I actually feel good about putting into my body. Instead of relying on artificial additives, it focuses on simple nutrition that fits with the way I already eat.

For me, that's exactly what I was looking for.

## I Want to Eat Real Food

Here's something important: I don't rely on protein powder every day because I think it's healthier than food.

Quite the opposite.

My goal is always to eat real, whole foods first.

Chicken. Fish. Eggs. Greek yogurt. Beans. Lentils. Those are still the foundation of my diet whenever my gut tolerates them.

Protein powder simply fills the gaps.

Whether I'm traveling, running between meetings, filming content all day, or just don't have time to cook, it's an easy way to increase my protein intake without grabbing highly processed convenience foods.

That's why ingredient quality matters so much to me.

## Supporting My Gut for the Long Run

Because of my history with IBS, liver issues, and my increased risk for colon cancer, I'm constantly thinking about the small choices that add up over time.

I'm never looking for a quick fix.

I'm looking for habits I can maintain for years.

Choosing products with simple ingredients is one of those habits.

Does that mean Organised Protein will heal IBS or prevent colon cancer? Absolutely not.

But it does help me stay consistent with eating enough protein while choosing ingredients that align with the way I want to nourish my body.

Consistency has always been far more powerful than perfection.
