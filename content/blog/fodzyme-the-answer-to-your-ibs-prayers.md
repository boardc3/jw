[FODZYME](https://partners.fodzyme.com/MINDFULJULIA) is a powdered enzyme supplement that breaks down FODMAPs, making them easier to digest. FODMAP stands for Fermentable Oligosaccharides, Disaccharides, Monosaccharides and Polyols, which are types of short-chain carbohydrates (sugars) that are poorly absorbed by the small intestine. FODMAP-intolerance can lead to symptoms such as bloating, gas, diarrhea, and constipation.

Even though a low-FODMAP diet is often effective in managing symptoms, it can be highly restrictive and have negative nutritional consequences. FODMAPs are in a ton of healthy fruits, vegetables and whole grains. Plus, FODMAPs, like GOS and fructans, are rich in prebiotic fibers and support short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) production, which are byproducts of FODMAP fermentation and provide many health benefits.

3. **Alpha-galactosidase**: Breaks down galacto-oligosaccharides (GOS), which are found in beans, lentils, and some vegetables.
