This liver-supporting, low FODMAP smoothie is my daily go-to. Starting my day with this protein-packed blend has helped me lose and maintain weight while improving my liver health. With my BYOS (Build Your Own Smoothie) template, you can make your favorite smoothies at home!

## Build Your Own Smoothie (BYOS)

Choose (1) of each to build your own smoothie!

### 1. Protein options:

- Plain Greek yogurt (unsweetened, nonfat recommended for liver/gallbladder issues)
- [Collagen peptides protein](https://amzn.to/3rMSyyX)
- [Organic Hemp Protein Powder](https://amzn.to/3Q9lHOV) - this vegan powder is an excellent option, packed with protein, iron, and calcium, delivering 290% of your daily iron, 20% of your calcium, and 100% of your potassium intake.

### 2. Greens options:

- Spinach - mild taste and texture
- Kale - easy to digest for IBS sufferers when blended

### 3. Chocolate:

- [Dutch process cocoa powder](https://amzn.to/3KhIpR0) - adds iron and an amazing flavor

### 4. **Fruit options** (choose one to avoid excessive sugar):

- (1) cup frozen blueberries (for taste)
- (1) unripe banana if you have IBS otherwise a ripe banana is fine
- (1) cup pineapple - low FODMAP, high in vitamin C
- (1) cup strawberries - low FODMAP, low sugar

### 5. Add-ins:

- (2) ice cubes - essential for smooth texture - DO NOT forget to add to your smoothie
- [Dash of organic cinnamon](https://amzn.to/3KhIpR0) (Ceylon is best - linked)
- Spirulina (nutrient-rich but strong taste)

### 6. Liquid options:

- *Add minimal liquid for a smoothie bar-style consistency*
- Coconut water - hydrating but not low FODMAP if you have IBS
- Almond milk - flavorful but watch for almond sensitivity (I can’t drink this but the taste is amazing)
- Carrot turmeric juice - love Trader Joe's brand

### 7. Fat options:

- *For those dealing with liver and gallbladder health issues, it may be necessary to restrict fat intake*
- [Walnuts](https://amzn.to/3q4F9BE) - great for brain function
- [Pumpkin seeds](https://amzn.to/3Qf8KTz) - nutrient-dense
- [Sunflower seeds](https://amzn.to/3KbPtif) - anti-inflammatory
- [Brazil nuts](https://amzn.to/3Ou1mTe) - two provide your daily Selenium intake
